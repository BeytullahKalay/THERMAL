## MARKDOWN PREVIEW (BBCode'siz, hızlı oku)

> *// 21:54 — UNIT 4 / NIGHT ROTATION*
> *You are OP.18. The previous operator left a handover note. Read it.*
> *The procedure manual is in the cabinet.*
> *It is correct for most error codes.*
> *It is not correct for all of them.*

**THERMAL** is a short, atmospheric night-shift simulation. You sit at a CRT terminal in front of a nuclear reactor for seven shifts. Each shift is roughly fifteen minutes of real time. By morning the system needs to be stable, the workers need to be alive, and you need enough wages to cover rent and your daughter's treatment.

The operator who sat in this chair before you died. You will find out why.

### KEY FEATURES

- **Cryptic error codes are the core puzzle.** Codes fire without warning. The procedure manual prescribes a fix for each one. Some prescriptions are wrong. Three operators figured this out before you. Two are dead. One file is redacted.
- **Ten named workers, ten unique stories.** ALEKSEI is six weeks into the program and engaged. IRINA is a single mother who asks to be home by seven. DMITRY was supposed to start engineering school in the autumn. You decide where to send them. Their dose stays on their record. If you push them too far, they don't come back the same.
- **A lying manual.** Most procedures work. Three don't. The operators who learned which three are no longer with the facility. Trust the cabinet and you'll meltdown. Trust the dead and you'll survive.
- **Post-shift reconstruction.** Every shift ends with a quiet panel showing what you reported next to what actually happened. The numbers don't match. They never do.
- **A news cycle that remembers.** Local papers report on the district downwind. Cluster of illnesses. Unidentified industrial fatality. Routine pediatric referrals. The articles never name the facility.
- **One ending that lands.** There are three outcomes. None of them is a victory screen. Two minutes of post-shift narrative play either way. The position will be filled.

### NIGHT SHIFT, NO HEROICS

THERMAL is for players who liked the bureaucratic weight of *Papers, Please*, the deductive density of *Return of the Obra Dinn*, and the moral attrition of *This War of Mine*. It is short — under three hours from first shift to last — and built to be played in one sitting.

Six languages on day one. Full keyboard rebinding. Authentic CRT scanline aesthetic that has been refined over hundreds of hours so it does not give you a headache.

The shift starts at 22:00. Your badge is on the desk.
