# THERMAL — Launch Marketing Plan

## 1. LAUNCH TRAILER (60–75 sec) — shot list

The trailer follows the "manual lies" hook because that is the single most shareable beat in the entire game. Most indie horror trailers fail by showing a feature montage; this one tells one story.

| Time | Shot | Sound |
|------|------|-------|
| 0:00 – 0:03 | Cold open. Black screen. CRT power-on flash. Title fades on for half a second: **THERMAL**. | Low hum, single CRT click |
| 0:03 – 0:10 | Tight on the gauges. POWER drifting. Operator hand reaches for + button. Calm. | Ambient room hum, faint sparks |
| 0:10 – 0:18 | First **ER-3505** alarm. AHN-AHN-AHN. Red overlay. Code stamps onscreen. | Alarm. Hard cut |
| 0:18 – 0:26 | Manual opens. Camera pushes into the printed prescription. Voice-over (text on screen): *"Manual says V1 → V2 → V3 → V4."* | Paper rustle, manual flick |
| 0:26 – 0:34 | Player executes the manual sequence. Reactor gauges go RED. Crit timer climbs. | Alarm rises in pitch, tremor sound |
| 0:34 – 0:42 | Cut to home terminal. KOWALSKI_OP14.LOG decrypts. Margin note types itself out: *"Reversed on instinct. V4 first. Loop steadied in seconds."* | Typewriter, dial-up tone |
| 0:42 – 0:52 | Back at reactor. New ER fires. Player runs the OPERATOR'S sequence (V4-V3-V1-V2). Loop stabilises. FIXED banner. | Alarm cuts. Single relief beat |
| 0:52 – 0:60 | Quick cuts: news article "POWER PLANT TECHNICIAN FOUND UNRESPONSIVE." Elena message. PREVIOUS_OP_FINAL.LOG. | Music low, dread-slow |
| 0:60 – 0:68 | Title card: **THERMAL** — *the manual is wrong on purpose* | Single deep tone |
| 0:68 – 0:75 | Steam logo + "WISHLIST NOW" / launch date | Silence then heartbeat |

**Total music:** one slow synth pad with metallic percussion. No voiceover narration. All dialogue is text from the actual game.

## 4. CURATOR PITCH (50 words, copy-paste)

> THERMAL is a slow-horror reactor sim where the procedure manual is wrong on purpose. You play a night-shift operator following safety procedures that previous operators left margin notes warning against. ~3-6 hours per run, multiple endings, full save persistence between deaths. Look at: Papers Please, Stories Untold, Iron Lung.
