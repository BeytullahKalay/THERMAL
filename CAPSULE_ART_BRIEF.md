## VISUAL DIRECTION

**Genre:** slow-horror reactor sim with bureaucratic dread
**Inspiration:** *Papers Please* capsule, *Iron Lung* capsule, *Stories Untold* capsule, Chernobyl HBO key art, declassified Soviet reactor poster art, pre-1990 industrial control room photography

**Color palette:**
- `#a8ff3e` — phosphor green (primary; CRT glow)
- `#ffb830` — amber (secondary; warning, paper)
- `#ff3a3a` — red alert (accent only — for ER code or alarm)
- `#080a04` — true black background
- `#0b0f05` — near-black panel surfaces

**Mood words:** lonely, lit by green CRT, cold-war industrial, paperwork, dread, late-night
**Avoid:** action poses, weapons, anime style, AAA-realism, photo-bashed assets, overly bright color

## CONCEPT — MAIN CAPSULE (the one that sells the game)

Single subject, instantly readable at 231×87. The image must communicate "horror + bureaucracy + reactor" in 0.5 seconds.

**Layout option A (recommended):**
- Foreground: silhouette of a lone operator from behind, sitting at a CRT terminal, head slightly tilted
- The CRT shows a single giant red error code: **ER-3505** (or similar)
- Smoke/fog rising from below the terminal, lit phosphor green
- The procedure manual is open on the desk to the operator's left, with one page visibly torn out
- Title **THERMAL** placed bottom-left or center-bottom in clean monospace, phosphor green
- Optional small text under title: "the manual is wrong on purpose"

**Layout option B (riskier, more iconic):**
- Tight close-up of the manual page (top-down), one prescription struck through in red ink, a hand-written correction in the margin
- Title overlaid in CRT phosphor green
- Single drop of coffee ring on the page corner

Either option works. **Option A is safer for visibility on Steam.**

- Title color: phosphor green `#a8ff3e` with subtle outer glow

## SMALL CAPSULE (231 × 87) NOTES

This is the hardest size — most indies fail it. The viewer sees this before clicking. Three rules:

1. The **title must be readable at thumbnail size**. If you can't read "THERMAL" at 231×87, the asset fails.
2. **One subject, no detail spam.** A single iconic visual + title. Resist the urge to cram a screenshot.
3. **High contrast.** Phosphor green on near-black is your friend.

Example: title centered, single ER code as background ghost element, that's it.

## OUTSOURCING TIPS

1. **Pay 50% upfront, 50% on final.** Standard.
2. **Two rounds of revisions included.** Specify in contract.
3. **Get the layered source file.** Critical for future updates and sales banners.
4. **Specify Steam capsule guidelines URL** in the brief: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets
5. **Ask for the small capsule first.** If they nail the hardest size, the rest follows.
