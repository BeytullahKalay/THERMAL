## ITERATION TIPS

1. **Generate 4–6 candidates per prompt.** Pick the one closest to mood; iterate with single-element tweaks.
2. **Don't generate the title text.** AI models butcher it. Use Figma / Photoshop / GIMP to overlay `THERMAL` in VT323 or Share Tech Mono font, phosphor green `#a8ff3e`, with outer glow.
3. **Test small.** Resize your candidate to 231×87 before committing. If "THERMAL" isn't readable at that size, the composition is wrong.
4. **Match the in-game palette exactly.** The HEX codes in each prompt are not negotiable — they're what every CRT effect in the game uses. The capsule should feel like a screenshot from the game.
5. **One subject per image.** No collage. No three-panel split. A single iconic visual + room for the title.

---

## POST-PROCESSING (after AI gen)

In Photoshop / GIMP / Figma:
1. Crop / extend to exact pixel dimensions per Steam guidelines
2. Add title text "THERMAL" in VT323 font, phosphor green, with outer glow + slight CRT chromatic aberration
3. Optional: overlay subtle scanline pattern at 8% opacity (matches in-game CRT)
4. Optional: add subtitle "the manual is wrong on purpose" in Share Tech Mono small underneath, amber color
5. Export PNG, sRGB, at exact Steam sizes (Steam compresses anyway but ship clean originals)
